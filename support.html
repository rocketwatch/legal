Contact

Email rocketwatchapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Including your device model and iOS version helps us help you faster.

Rocket Watch Crew subscription

Rocket Watch is free to browse. The optional Rocket Watch Crew subscription adds timed push alerts before launches. New users get Crew notifications free for their first 30 days.

To manage, cancel, or restore a subscription, open Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Restore Purchase on the Crew screen in the app. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple through your Apple ID.

Notifications

If launch alerts aren't arriving, make sure notifications are enabled for Rocket Watch in Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Rocket Watch, and that you have an active Crew subscription (or are within your free trial).

Privacy

Rocket Watch collects no personal data and does not use your device location. See our Privacy Policy for details.
