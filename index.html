🪼 Rocket Watch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Rocket Watch is a launch-tracking app for the Space Coast. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. In short: Rocket Watch does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. Rocket Watch has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, device identifiers, or usage data.

Location

Rocket Watch does not request or access your device's location. All launch, weather, and sky-condition information is fixed to the Cape Canaveral / Kennedy Space Center area (the Space Coast). Your personal location is never read or transmitted.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Rocket Watch schedules launch-alert reminders locally on your device. These notifications are generated on-device and are not sent through, or routed to, any server we operate.

Purchases

The optional "Rocket Watch Crew" subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Rocket Watch never sees or stores your payment details. Your subscription status is managed by Apple and read on-device via Apple's StoreKit. Manage or cancel any time in Settings ▸ Apple ID ▸ Subscriptions.

Third-party services

To show upcoming launches, weather, and daylight data, the app requests public information from the following services. These requests contain no personal data beyond the standard network information (such as your IP address) inherent to any internet connection, which each provider handles under its own policy:

  • The Space Devs — Launch Library (launch schedules)
  • Open-Meteo (weather and cloud-cover forecasts)
  • Sunrise/sunset data provider (daylight timing)

Children

Rocket Watch is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data retention

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Any app settings (such as your first-launch date used for the free-trial window) are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
